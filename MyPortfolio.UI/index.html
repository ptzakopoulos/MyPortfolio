01 / About

Started in the frontend, grew into the full stack.

I began as a Frontend Developer at Threenitas and learned the backend on the job, working in .NET and Umbraco. Today I move a Figma design all the way to a deployed, production site: pixel-accurate HTML and CSS, TypeScript on the client, and the controllers, services and content models behind it.

I use AI tooling such as Cursor, with MCP integrations for Figma and Blender, to work faster, and I always review the result and run the tests before it ships. I’m studying Informatics at the Hellenic Open University, I’m open to relocation, and I want to keep learning new technologies.

I have a solid grounding in UI and UX, and I work directly with project managers, designers and backend developers, and sometimes with the client, to find the right solution together.
